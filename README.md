# Koshimbir

Serve `dist/` as a static site. For a local preview, run `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist` from the project root and open `http://localhost:8000`. Fonts, brand artwork and editorial photographs are self-hosted.

## Editing the experience

- Keep the section anchors and meal radio values in `dist/index.html` stable. They serve direct links and existing, opt-in saved plans.
- The hero uses the original hand, cup and dressing choreography in `dist/pour.js` and `dist/pour-data.js`. The photograph, curved wordmark, single-piece hand and dressing share a 1536 × 1024 coordinate system and one camera push. Scroll position drives the pour in both directions. The closing headline holds at the end of the pour and the opening returns at the top. Reduced motion and data saver use the opening still.
- The ingredient buttons, tasting notes and their matching coordinates feed `dist/fork.js`. They refer to the physical features of the photographed forkful.
- The office and post-training scenes in `dist/editions.js` use native horizontal scrolling. The same script turns the philosophy pages and prepares email drafts. The first spread is rendered in the HTML. On a page turn the photograph updates immediately and six hinged strips briefly carry its new image. Add a philosophy article and its matching button together. The contact link also works with JavaScript disabled.
- The carrier opens before Meal Plans and takes three separate paths to the radio labels. Drag it or use the arrow keys to turn it around its vertical axis. `dist/passage.js` drives the same timeline for the lazy Three.js scene and the offline-rendered, 32-angle fallback in assets/turntable. Each on-demand angle contains five 1024-pixel cells. The fallback composites each piece separately and draws a fresh crop when its angle or display resolution changes. The packed carrier turns slowly. Hover lifts a landed serving and speeds up its rotation. The selected plan keeps turning after the pointer leaves. Reduced motion and data-saving mode use stills. To edit the model, install the two build dependencies with `npm install --no-save three@0.182.0 esbuild@0.25.12`. From the project root, rebuild the browser module after changing its source with `node_modules/.bin/esbuild dist/source/tiffin-model.js --bundle --format=esm --minify --legal-comments=none --target=es2020 --outfile=dist/tiffin-gl.js`.
- Plan data lives on the native radios. Each is associated with a label under its carrier and a rotation and selection button on the carrier. `dist/planner.js` prepares the meal enquiry with the chosen plan, duration, time, dietary preference and optional extras. The current menu remains on koshimbir.com.
- `dist/style.css` and `dist/kitchen.css` define the brand typography, hero, fork and meal configurator. `dist/courtyard.css` and `dist/passage.css` define the story frame, connected carrier scene, field book and contact controls. `dist/studio.css` supplies the forest-green product studio, plan shelf, depth-layered ambient light and individually choreographed headlines. `dist/app.js` manages navigation.

The food and lifestyle scenes are editorial illustrations. The family table photograph appears in the philosophy book. The enamel carrier uses the original geometry and opening choreography in `dist/source/tiffin-model.js`, with sculpted, textured salad ingredients inside its three tiers. Fonts and Three.js licences are in `dist/licenses/`.
